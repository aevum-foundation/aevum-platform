# Aevum Privacy Policy — Content Draft v1

**Status:** Draft
**Effective date:** 17 September 2026
**Last updated:** 17 September 2026
**Based on:** Production Data Audit (2026-09-17)

---

## 1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Aevum web platform
(available at aevumchain.com) collects, uses, and protects
information when you use the website.

It does not describe the Aevum protocol itself. The protocol
is decentralized infrastructure and operates independently
of the website.

---

## 2. Scope and Applicability

This policy applies to:

- The Aevum web platform (aevumchain.com).
- Account features offered through the platform.

This policy does not apply to:

- The Aevum protocol (decentralized network).
- Third-party services linked from the website (e.g., GitHub,
  Telegram).
- Any external site or service you reach through links on
  the platform.

Third-party services are governed by their own terms and
privacy policies.

---

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address.
- Authentication credential information (stored in a securely
  hashed form).

### 3.2 Authentication and Security Information

When you sign in or use account features, we may collect:

- Session information.
- Security-related events (such as login failures, password
  changes, and rate-limit triggers).
- Information used for abuse prevention.

### 3.3 Profile and Preferences

If you use profile or preference features, we may store:

- Display name and profile information you provide.
- Preferences such as theme and language.

### 3.4 Technical Information

When you access the website, our servers may record:

- IP address.
- User-agent (browser and device information).
- Timestamps.
- Requested URLs.

This information is collected for security, operational
integrity, and abuse prevention.

---

## 4. Information We Do Not Require

The Aevum web platform is designed not to require users to
submit private keys or recovery phrases to the platform.

Users should never submit private keys or recovery phrases
through website forms, support channels, or community
services.

The platform does not require wallet data to use account
features.

---

## 5. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and operate account features.
- Authenticate users and maintain sessions.
- Protect the platform from abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
- Maintain the security and integrity of the service.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use this information for advertising.

---

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage

The platform uses the following:

### Strictly necessary

- **Session cookie** — used to maintain your authenticated session.
- **CSRF token** — used to protect against cross-site request forgery.

These are required for the platform to function. They are not
used for tracking or advertising.

### Local browser storage

- **Theme preference** — stored in your browser's local storage
  to remember your selected theme (dark or light).

Local storage is not a cookie. It is a separate browser
mechanism.

---

## 7. Data Storage and Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to
protect the information we hold.

These include:

- Secure authentication mechanisms.
- Session protection.
- Rate limiting and abuse prevention.
- Security event monitoring.

No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute
security of any information.

---

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Aevum web platform links to third-party services for
development and community purposes.

These include:

- **GitHub** — used for source code, repositories, and
  development infrastructure.
- **Telegram** — used as an external community platform.

When you follow links to these services, your interaction
with them is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not control how these third-party services collect or
process your information.

---

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary
for the purposes described in this policy.

Specifically:

- **Session data** — retained until logout, expiry, or until
  it is no longer required.
- **Security and audit data** — retained as reasonably
  necessary for security, abuse prevention, investigation,
  and accountability.
- **Technical and server logs** — retained according to
  operational requirements and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize
it in accordance with our operational practices.

---

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under
applicable data protection laws, including:

- **Access** — request a copy of the information we hold
  about you.
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate
  information.
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your information.
- **Restriction or objection** — request restriction of, or
  object to, certain processing.
- **Portability** — request transfer of your information
  where applicable.
- **Complaint** — lodge a complaint with the relevant
  supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in
Section 13.

These rights are not absolute. Some may be subject to legal
or operational limitations.

---

## 11. International Data Transfers

The Aevum web platform may use infrastructure located in
different jurisdictions.

Where information is transferred across borders, we take
reasonable steps to ensure it is protected in accordance
with applicable law.

---

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we do:

- The **Effective date** at the top of this page will be
  updated.
- Material changes will be reflected in the policy text.

Continued use of the platform after changes take effect
means you accept the updated policy.
