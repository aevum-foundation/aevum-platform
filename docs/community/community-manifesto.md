# Aevum Manifesto

> Aevum strives to turn idle computing resources into open, global compute infrastructure.

---

## The Problem

Compute has become critical infrastructure. Scientific research, machine learning, simulation, cryptography, and discovery all depend on it.

Today, critical compute capacity is increasingly concentrated in large centralized providers. Independent researchers, small teams, and entire regions can be priced out of compute they could otherwise use.

At the same time, enormous amounts of compute sit idle — in homes, universities, laboratories, and small businesses.

Aevum exists to address this imbalance.

---

## What Aevum Is

Aevum is a decentralized compute infrastructure.

It connects independent devices — GPUs, CPUs, and other hardware — into a shared network where useful computational work can be distributed, executed, and verified.

Aevum strives to turn idle computing resources into open, global compute infrastructure.

Blockchain is used as coordination infrastructure. It is not the purpose of Aevum. The purpose is the compute network.

---

## What Aevum Is Not

Aevum is not another blockchain.

Aevum is not a company.

Aevum is not a way to earn tokens.

Aevum does not promise wealth, employment, or a specific financial outcome.

Aevum is an open engineering project. What it produces is real infrastructure, real code, and real results.

---

## What We Believe

**Open.** The technology is developed in the open, so that it can be studied, audited, and improved.

**Decentralized.** No single data center, company, or organization is the foundation of the network.

**Useful.** Compute is directed toward work with practical value — not toward pointless energy expenditure.

**Voluntary.** Participation is a choice. Nobody owes Aevum their time, hardware, or attention.

**Composable.** Anyone can build on top of the protocol. Aevum defines primitives, not a closed platform.

**Real.** Where results are not yet proven, we say so. Where claims cannot be measured, we do not make them.

---

## Who This Is For

Aevum is for anyone who wants to learn, build, or contribute to decentralized compute infrastructure.

Students. Developers. Researchers. Cryptographers. Hardware enthusiasts. Contributors of any kind. Anyone curious.

You do not need permission to participate. You do not need a formal background. You do not need to be a member of any organization.

You can contribute one pull request, run one node, write one article, learn one concept, or simply observe. All of these are valid ways to participate.

---

## What You Can Do

**Learn.** [Learn v1](https://aevumchain.com/learn) describes a path from fundamentals to contribution — starting from computer science, Rust, cryptography, and distributed systems.

**Contribute.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to open a first issue, make a first pull request, and get help.

**Build.** Write a workload. Improve a node. Publish research. Contribute documentation. Every kind of contribution matters.

**Explore.** Not everyone needs to contribute. Reading the documentation, following the project, and understanding what decentralized compute infrastructure is — all of this is welcome.

---

## Closing

A bee does not build a giant factory. It moves between many places, gathers small amounts of resource, and returns them to a shared system. Many independent bees can produce a result larger than any single bee could produce alone.

Aevum works the same way.

One GPU is a resource. Millions of independent GPUs become infrastructure.

Aevum is built by people who choose to build it.
