GUIDE

How to Ask a Good Question

Four things that help others help you.

Explain what you are trying to do

Describe your goal, not just the error. A clear goal makes it easier to suggest the right solution.

Describe what happened instead

What did you expect? What did you get? Be specific about the difference.

Include logs, errors, or screenshots when relevant

Paste the exact error message. Include the command you ran. Small details often matter.

Explain what you already tried

This avoids repeating suggestions you have already tested, and shows that you have done your part.
