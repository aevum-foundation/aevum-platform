WHY

Why Contribute

Aevum is built in public. The project grows through the work of contributors who choose to participate — not through status, credentials, or employment.

Contribution is measured by work, not by title. Help is needed in many areas: code, documentation, testing, research, and community support. Each of these improves the project and builds trust over time.

See Learn v1 §6 for how contribution becomes trust.

PROCESS

How It Works

Four steps from first issue to merged contribution.

Find an issue

Look for labels like good first issue, help wanted, or level:beginner. You can pick any issue you feel ready for.

Open a pull request

Fork the repository, make a focused change, run the relevant checks, and open a PR. Describe what changed, why, and how it was verified.

Get review

A reviewer may ask questions or suggest changes. This is normal. Review is a conversation, not a gate.

Merge

When the change is ready, a maintainer merges it. Your contribution becomes part of the project.

WHAT

What You Can Contribute

Eight directions. Code is one of them. You do not need to be a Rust developer to have a place in Aevum.

Documentation

Guides, specifications, corrections, and examples that help others understand the project.

Testing

Reproducing bugs, writing test coverage, running manual and integration tests.

Rust Development

Features, fixes, refactors, and tooling in the Rust codebase.

Cryptography Research

Post-quantum primitives, envelope design, and cryptographic review.

Protocol Design

Consensus, settlement, verification, and economic design.

Compute / GPU

Workload packaging, execution, and verification across distributed nodes.

Developer Experience

Tooling, SDKs, CI, and improvements that make contribution easier.

Community Support

Helping newcomers, answering questions, and keeping spaces usable.

PREREQUISITES

What You Do NOT Need

To contribute to Aevum, you do not need:

No degree

No credential is required to learn or contribute.

No permission

You don't need to be accepted, invited, or approved by anyone.

No prior contribution history

Your first contribution can be your first contribution.

No payment

Contribution is voluntary. There is no paid tier or gated content.

No long-term commitment

You can make one PR and stop, or contribute for years.
