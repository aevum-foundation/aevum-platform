The foundation of the network

Aevum begins with a fixed genesis allocation and a protocol designed around post-quantum security, equal participation and epoch-native finality.

Foundational Principles

The core ideas established at the origin of the Aevum protocol.

01 / MONETARY

Genesis Supply

21,000,000 AEV are established at genesis. The protocol defines a maximum supply of 371,000,000 AEV.

02 / SECURITY

Post-Quantum

Aevum is designed around post-quantum cryptographic primitives, including ML-KEM, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA.

03 / PARTICIPATION

Proof of Presence

Network participation is designed around presence and fair participation rather than energy-intensive mining.
