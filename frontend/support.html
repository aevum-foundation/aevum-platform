Support Aevum

Support the protocol.

Help sustain the open-source development of Aevum. Contributions are voluntary and are not a purchase of AEV, an investment, or a promise of future value.

Principles

Open support. No entitlement.

01 / VOLUNTARY

Support is optional.

Contributions are entirely voluntary. Access to the Aevum protocol does not depend on making a contribution.

02 / NO ALLOCATION

Support does not buy AEV.

A contribution does not purchase AEV, reserve an allocation, grant protocol rights, or create an entitlement to receive tokens.

Purpose

Help sustain the work.

01 / DEVELOPMENT

Protocol development

Core protocol engineering, infrastructure, testing and long-term maintenance.

02 / RESEARCH

Security & research

Cryptography research, security engineering, protocol analysis and scalability work.

03 / ECOSYSTEM

Open ecosystem

Documentation, developer resources and tools that make the network easier to understand and use.
